## Summary

Two or three sentences: what hidden or unused content is known for this game,
and how much of it was checked in an actual build and in the actual game.

## Known content

One subsection per item. For each, keep the four statements separate:

- Present in the data: yes / no / unknown, with the location (container and internal path).
- Possibly unused: yes / no / unknown, with the references checked and their coverage.
- Loaded under test conditions: yes / no / unknown, with what was executed and observed.
- Reachable in normal play: yes / no / unknown, only from an executed normal route.
- Release history: status, scope and the checked sources.
